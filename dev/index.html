    Tips

    • Click on the book, chapter, or verse selector to jump to any location.
    • Search by reference to jump quickly (examples: John 3, John 3:16, jn 3 16).
    • Search by keywords to find mentions across the Bible (example: covenant).
    • Search for an exact phrase by wrapping it in double quotes (example: "in love").
    • Enable "Search all translations" in the search bar to find verses unique to other translations.
    • Use Settings to change font size, display options, theme, and translation.
    • Your reading position and settings sync across devices when signed in.

    Getting Started

    • Sign Up: Create an account to sync your settings and reading position across devices.
    • Customize: Adjust font size, toggle verse numbers, headings, and translation in Settings.
    • Search: Use search to jump directly to references or find words and phrases across the selected translation.
