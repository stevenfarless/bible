About the Deuterocanon

The deuterocanonical books (also called the Apocrypha in Protestant traditions) are writings included in the Catholic and Eastern Orthodox biblical canons but not in the Hebrew Bible or most Protestant Old Testament canons.


These books were part of the Septuagint (the Greek translation of the Hebrew scriptures) and were used by early Christians. The Council of Trent (1546) formally defined them as canonical for the Catholic Church.


Books included: Tobit, Judith, 1 Maccabees, 2 Maccabees, Wisdom of Solomon, Sirach (Ecclesiasticus), and Baruch (including the Letter of Jeremiah).


Enabling this option adds these books to the book selection list when a compatible translation is loaded.
