    About the Apocrypha / Deuterocanon

    The Deuterocanon (also called the Apocrypha) refers to a collection of Jewish texts written between roughly 300 BC and 100 AD. They are included in Catholic and Orthodox Old Testament canons but are not part of the Hebrew Bible or most Protestant canons.

    These books are shown here because the selected translation includes them.
